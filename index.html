Our story

Packaging for the Kenya we want to keep.

In August 2017, Kenya banned plastic bags. For many people it felt like a door closing. Shopkeepers, market traders and small businesses suddenly had nothing to carry their goods in. I saw an opening instead.

I have always loved nature: the green of a clean riverbank, soil that drinks the rain. Qapubags began with one simple idea: if people need packaging, let it be packaging the earth can take back.

After years in banking, I know a business with a mission still has to run on sound numbers. That discipline lets us keep prices fair for small businesses, so more people can switch away from plastic without it costing them their margins.

Small moments

That remind us why.

The first order

A cosmetics customer who needed packaging after the ban, and was relieved to find a local, biodegradable option.

The first print

A customer seeing their own logo on a finished bag for the first time, their colours and idea coming to life.

The question

"Will this really break down?" That question now guides how we explain every product.

The ripple

A repeat customer who switched fully to biodegradable packaging and started telling others.
