# Images

- `logo.png` / `logo-white.png`: header and footer wordmark, cut from `docs/Qapubags Logo.pdf`
- `products/linda-bright-were.jpeg`: founder photo, from the company profile

All other photos on the site are **free Pexels placeholders** (pexels.com/license),
loaded straight from images.pexels.com. Replace them with Qapubags' own photos before launch:
save the photo here (e.g. `products/kraft-bags.jpg`) and change that image's `src` in `index.html`.

Photo guide for her shots: plain light background or natural surface (wood, linen),
daylight from a window, one product per photo, portrait framing (4:5),
at least 1200 px wide, compressed under ~300 KB (squoosh.app).
